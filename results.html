How these are graded

A projection is logged before kickoff and compared afterwards against the closing market number on the same game. Against the spread counts whether the model landed on the correct side of that number; straight up counts only whether it named the winner, which is the easier question and is shown next to what picking every market favourite would have returned.

Week 4 logged a projected margin; projected scores begin in week 5, so total-points accuracy is reported from week 5 onward.

Records at this sample size are mostly noise, and the page prints each one's confidence interval so that is visible rather than implied. The column worth following is the average margin miss against the market's on identical games. This is a description of how the model has performed, not betting advice.

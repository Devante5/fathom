Efficiency — opponent-adjusted

Data through Week 3

Every number here is adjusted for who the team actually played. A rating is what the offence or defence is worth per play against an average opponent, in expected points added. Higher is better in both columns — a big offence rating scores, a big defence rating suppresses. This is the view to rank on; the other two are raw.

Advanced — raw per-game rates

Data through Week 3

Per-game averages straight from the box score, not adjusted for opponent. A team that has played three overmatched opponents will look better here than it is. For defensive columns lower is better; for offensive columns higher is.

Traditional — points and yards

Data through Week 4

Counting stats, per game, not adjusted for opponent. Included because people want to see them, not because they rank teams well — the Efficiency tab does that job.

Reading these tabs

Efficiency is the only opponent-adjusted view, and the only one that should be used to compare teams with different schedules. Advanced and Traditional are raw: a team that has played three cupcakes looks better on them than it is. Each tab states the week its data runs through, because they do not all refresh at the same time — results land immediately, the advanced box scores need their own pull.

No betting lines are used anywhere on this page. Rank and Score come from the Fathom team rating; everything else is descriptive.
